Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This describes what data Ledger collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password (used only for account login)
  • Account and transaction data from banks you connect via Plaid (balances, transaction history, account names)
  • Anything you manually enter (assets, liabilities, goals, checklist selections)
  • Basic usage of the app itself (which features you use), stored locally on your device, not sent to us

What we don't collect

Ledger never sees or stores your bank login credentials. That connection is handled entirely by Plaid, a third-party financial data provider used by thousands of finance apps.

How your data is used

Your data is used solely to power the features you see in the app (net worth calculation, spending breakdowns, forecasts, and similar tools). It is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers.

Where your data is stored

Your account data is stored in a Supabase database, accessible only to your logged-in account. Bank connection tokens are currently stored without additional encryption beyond standard database security; this is being improved as Ledger moves past early testing.

Third parties

Ledger uses Plaid to connect bank accounts and StockData.org to look up stock prices. Each has its own privacy practices, governing the data that passes through them.

Your control over your data

You can disconnect any bank account at any time from the Manage tab. To request full account deletion, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Ledger develops. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
