FAQ
Common questions about how Ledger works.
Is my data safe? +
Ledger connects to your accounts through Plaid, the same connection technology used by thousands of financial apps. Ledger never sees or stores your bank login — Plaid handles that directly. The connection is read-only: nothing in this app can move money or make changes to your accounts.
What is the financial health score? +
A single 0-100 number combining four things: how many months your liquid cash would cover your fixed costs (runway), how much of your spending is discretionary versus fixed (elasticity), how leveraged you are (debt versus assets), and whether your net worth is trending up or down. It's a starting formula, not a certified financial rating.
Why don't I see a score yet? +
The score needs some transaction history to calculate elasticity and runway. Give it a few days after connecting a bank for that data to build up.
Can this move or send money? +
No. This is read-only. It can see balances and transactions, nothing more.
Resources
Understanding your runway
Runway is how many months your liquid cash would last if you stopped earning today, covering only fixed costs. A longer runway means more room to handle a shock or take a risk.
Discretionary vs. fixed spending
Fixed costs (rent, minimum debt payments, insurance) are hard to cut quickly. Discretionary spending (dining, entertainment, shopping) is where you actually have flexibility if things get tight.
Why net worth, not income
Income shows what's coming in. Net worth shows what you've actually built — everything you own minus everything you owe. It's a more complete picture for big decisions.
